Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

"The Void Underground Uploader" is a private tool used exclusively by the owner of the YouTube channel The Void Underground to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

The app requests only the youtube.upload permission for the owner's own Google account. It does not access, collect or store data of any other user.

Use and sharing

The authorization is used only to upload videos and their titles and descriptions to the owner's channel. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and revocation

The authorization token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private automation environment. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
